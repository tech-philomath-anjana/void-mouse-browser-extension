# Void Mouse Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 7 January 2026

Void Mouse processes video locally to detect hand landmarks for cursor control. No personal data, video, or gesture data is collected, stored, or sent to servers. Settings (colors, threshold, enabled state) are stored locally via `chrome.storage`. The extension does not sell or share data with third parties.

## Data collection
- **Video and gestures:** Processed on-device only for hand landmark detection; not collected, stored, or transmitted.

- **Settings:** Stored locally in the browser via `chrome.storage` for convenience.

## Sharing and selling
- Void Mouse does not sell or share data with third parties.

## Security
- Processing is local-first; no server communication is required for core functionality.
